# Decision

One declarative sentence: what is now decided.

# Context

The problem that forced the decision. State the door type: **one-way**
(irreversible, or costly to reverse — this is what justifies an ADR) or
**two-way** (reversible — though rarely for free).

# Decision Drivers

- Force or constraint 1 (deadline, cost, compliance, strategy, team…)
- Criterion 2 — what a good option must satisfy

# Options considered

- **Option A** — why not.
- **Option B (chosen)** — why.

# Consequences

What this commits us to, what it closes, what to watch.
