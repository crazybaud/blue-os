# Decision

The organization runs its actions, decisions, knowledge and journal in this
Obsidian vault, co-edited by humans and LLMs.

# Context

Two-way door: reversible by exporting the .md files. A decision only deserves
a file here when its scope outlives the current action — otherwise it lives
in that action's History.

# Consequences

- Actions are created through the form (Cmd+Shift+A), never by hand.
- The Journal is write-once; extraction feeds the other objects.
